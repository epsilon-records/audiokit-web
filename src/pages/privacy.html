Visiting audiokit.ai

Hosting and fonts

The site is served by Netlify. As with any website, your browser sends its IP address and request details (such as the page requested, browser type and referring page) to the host, which keeps them in its server logs.

Pages on audiokit.ai and in the console load their typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). Your browser therefore connects to Google and sends it your IP address and browser details when you open a page.

Error monitoring

Public pages load Sentry's browser script, which reports errors that happen in your browser on our pages and timing data for a sample of page loads. A report can include the page address, browser and operating system, and the IP address it was sent from.

Analytics

When it is switched on, selected public content pages of audiokit.ai use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure aggregate page traffic and page performance. It uses no cookies and no browser storage, and is not used to follow you across other websites. It receives the page address, the referring address and browser performance measurements. It is not used in the console, the API or the billing service, and it skips pages whose address contains a query string or fragment.

Where you came from

To learn which channels bring people to AudioKit, the page you land on reads three things: the name of the website that linked you to us (the host name only, such as news.example.com, never the full address), any utm_ campaign tags in the link, and the path of that page. It adds them to that page's links to sign up and sign in. Nothing is stored on your device for this: no cookie and no browser storage. If you move to another page on the site first, the values are not carried over.

If you follow one of those links and create an account, the values are stored with your account (see your account). If you do not create an account, nothing is stored on our side.

Your account and the console

Signing in

You sign up and sign in with your email address. We email you a single-use sign-in link that expires after 15 minutes. We keep only a cryptographic hash of each link, with when it was created, used and expires. There is no password.

Once you are signed in, the console sets one cookie, ak_console_session. It keeps you signed in, cannot be read by scripts on the page, and expires after 8 hours. The console needs it to work. The console runs no analytics script.

What we keep about you

  • Account: email address, name, and company if you give one; when the address was verified; account status; when the account was created and last signed in.
  • Where you came from: if you arrived through the site, the referring host name, utm_ tags and landing path described above, with the time of signup. These are set once, when the account is created, and never updated. If you arrive at the console directly, the host name of the page that linked to it (from your browser's Referer header) may be used instead. Internet addresses, email addresses and full URLs are discarded.
  • API keys: a hash of each key (never the key itself after it is shown to you once), its name, its first characters for display, and when it was created, last used and revoked.
  • Plan and usage: your plan, billing periods, and counts of jobs by operation per period.
  • Email preferences: whether you want quota warnings, payment receipts and product updates. Product updates are off unless you turn them on.
  • Payments: if you subscribe to a paid plan, your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, invoice records (amounts, tax, status, period, and a link to the invoice at Stripe), and the subscription events Stripe sends us. Card details are entered on Stripe's own page; we never see or store your card number.

Email we send

We send sign-in links, quota warnings, a notice when a free quota is used up, and payment receipts. They are sent through Resend from mail.audiokit.ai. Resend processes your address and the message content to deliver them.

Internal reports

We count, from our own account database, how many accounts go from signup to a first API key, a first job, a first stem job and a paid plan, together with weekly usage. The results are aggregate numbers emailed to the team once a week. No tracking script is involved.

Using the API

When you or your software submits a job, we keep a record of it: the operation and its settings, the input URL, file name and checksum you send, the output and webhook URLs you give us, the authorization field if you set one, the job's status and timings, its result (for example measured loudness, tempo or tags, and checksums of the files we uploaded), and any error. We also keep a record of each webhook we deliver. Treat input and output URLs as shared with us: if they are signed links, they work until they expire.

Audio and other files. AudioKit downloads each input from the URL you send. It processes the file in a temporary working directory that is deleted when processing ends, and uploads results directly to the upload URLs you provide. Stem separation runs on GPUs provided by Modal in the EU: Modal's service downloads the input from your URL, separates it and uploads each stem to your URLs, and keeps no copy. We do not keep copies of your audio, and we do not use it to train models.

Rate limits and security. To protect the service, we count requests per API key and per IP address. The counts are held in memory for a short window and are not stored. Our services also write operational logs of requests, which can include the IP address and the path requested.

Errors. When something fails in the API or the billing service, an error report goes to Sentry. It can include details of the request being handled.
