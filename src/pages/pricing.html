Usage-based pricing details

What counts as a job?

Every operation you submit counts as one job, regardless of the audio file size or processing time. Whether you're analyzing tempo, transcoding, or generating fingerprints—each request is one job. A retry that reuses the same job ID is not counted twice.

Stem separation is the one GPU-heavy operation, so it has its own allowance inside your job quota. A stem separation uses one slot from each allowance — it counts as one job and one stem — but it is charged once, at the stem rate.

Overage pricing

On Pro, overage is invoiced at the end of the billing period: $0.15 for each stem beyond your stem allowance, and $0.01 for each other job beyond your job allowance. A stem that is over the stem allowance is billed at $0.15 only — the $0.01 job rate does not also apply to it, and no unit is ever charged twice. Free tier users will need to upgrade to continue processing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens when I reach my job limit?
On the Free plan, processing stops until the next billing cycle or you upgrade. On Pro, we bill overage at the end of the period: $0.15 per stem over the stem allowance and $0.01 per other job over the job allowance. A stem is billed once, never as both a stem and a job. Enterprise plans have volume rates in their agreement.
Do I pay the job rate and the stem rate for one separation?
No. Stems use a slot from both allowances, so the separation is counted twice, but it is billed once — at $0.15 for a stem over your stem allowance, with no $0.01 job charge on top.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits or education?
Yes! We offer special pricing for verified non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Contact us to learn more.
Can I try AudioKit before choosing a paid plan?
The Free plan is the best way to try AudioKit, with 100 jobs per month and no credit card required. Pro starts as a paid subscription at checkout. Enterprise plans are sales-assisted—contact us to get started.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and bank transfers for Enterprise customers.
Are there any setup fees or long-term contracts?
No setup fees. Free and Pro plans are month-to-month with no commitment. Enterprise plans have flexible contract terms.
What's included in the 99.9% uptime SLA?
Pro and Enterprise plans include guaranteed uptime with service credits if we don't meet our commitment. See our full SLA documentation for details.
