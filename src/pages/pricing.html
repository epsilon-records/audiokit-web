Frequently asked questions

How much is a credit?
One credit is $0.01. A three-minute stem separation, for example, costs 9 credits, but the 10-credit minimum per job applies, so it is charged 10.
When can I buy a credit pack?
Not yet. AudioKit is in beta and packs are not on sale yet. You can sign up for the Free plan today.
Do credits expire?
Free credits lapse at the end of each calendar month (UTC). Credits from a pack stay valid for 12 months after you buy it.
Which credits are spent first?
Your monthly Free credits, then credits from packs, then any credits granted to you. A pack can expire unused while your Free credits are spent first.
How are per-minute jobs charged?
A job is accepted when your balance covers the operation's minimum. When it finishes, it is charged for the audio's real length. One long job can take your balance below zero; new jobs are then refused until you top up. A negative balance is repaid from the next credits you get, including your monthly Free credits.
Can I get a refund on a credit pack?
No. Credit packs are non-refundable.
Is there a subscription or contract?
No. There are no subscriptions, setup fees or long-term contracts. You pay for credit packs when you need them.
