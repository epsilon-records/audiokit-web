Usage-based pricing details

What counts as a job?

Every operation you submit counts as one job, regardless of the audio file size or processing time. Whether you're analyzing tempo, transcoding, or generating fingerprints—each request is one job. A retry that reuses the same job ID is not counted twice.

Stem separation is the one GPU-heavy operation, so it has its own allowance inside your job quota. A stem separation counts as one job and one stem, and each plan caps stems separately.

Overage pricing

On Pro, jobs beyond your monthly limit are billed at $0.01 each, and stem separations beyond your stem allowance at $0.15 each. Overage is invoiced at the end of the billing period. Free tier users will need to upgrade to continue processing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
What happens when I reach my job limit?
On the Free plan, processing stops until the next billing cycle or you upgrade. On Pro, we bill overages at the end of the period: $0.01 per job and $0.15 per stem separation. Enterprise plans have volume rates in their agreement.
Do you offer discounts for non-profits or education?
Yes! We offer special pricing for verified non-profit organizations and educational institutions. Contact us to learn more.
Is there a free trial for Pro or Enterprise?
All new users start on the Free plan with 100 jobs per month—no credit card required. This lets you thoroughly test AudioKit before upgrading. Pro also comes with a 14-day free trial; a card is required and you can cancel before it ends.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express) and bank transfers for Enterprise customers.
Are there any setup fees or long-term contracts?
No setup fees. Free and Pro plans are month-to-month with no commitment. Enterprise plans have flexible contract terms.
What's included in the 99.9% uptime SLA?
Pro and Enterprise plans include guaranteed uptime with service credits if we don't meet our commitment. See our full SLA documentation for details.
