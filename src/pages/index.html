How a job runs

  1. 1 · Submit

    One request per job

    Your job_id is the idempotency key. Retry freely: the same request never runs twice.

  2. 2 · Verify

    Checked before it runs

    AudioKit downloads from your signed URL and rejects any file whose sha256 doesn't match.

  3. 3 · Process

    Originals untouched

    New files go to upload URLs you give it. Results carry the input hash, AudioKit version and settings.

  4. 4 · Deliver

    Signed webhooks

    HMAC-signed events, retried for hours if your endpoint is down. The job is always one GET away.
