Privacy and analytics

{{ANALYTICS_STATUS}}

When enabled, the beacon receives page and referring URL information and browser performance measurements. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or browser storage or track people across customer sites.

The integration is limited to selected public content pages on audiokit.ai. It skips documents with query strings, fragments, sensitive or unknown paths, and unsafe original navigation or referring URLs. The customer console, API and billing service are outside this public-site integration.

Read Cloudflare’s explanation of its analytics beacon for details.
