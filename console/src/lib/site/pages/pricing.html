Frequently asked questions

How much is a credit?
One credit is $0.01. A three-minute stem separation, for example, costs 9 credits, but the 10-credit minimum per job applies, so it is charged 10.
When can I buy a credit pack?
Not yet. AudioKit is in beta and packs are not on sale yet. You can sign up for the Free plan today.
Do credits expire?
Free credits lapse at the end of each calendar month (UTC). Credits from a pack don't expire.
Which credits are spent first?
Your monthly Free credits, then credits from packs, then any credits granted to you. Pack credits are spent oldest first.
How are per-minute jobs charged?
A job is accepted when your balance covers the operation's minimum. Once the worker has measured the audio, the job is checked against its full cost for the real length before the expensive step runs. If your balance can't cover it, the job fails with insufficient_credits and isn't charged. When a job finishes, it's charged for the audio's real length, never more than the amount held for it. Your balance never goes below zero.
Can I get a refund on a credit pack?
No. Credit packs are non-refundable.
Is there a subscription or contract?
No. There are no subscriptions, setup fees or long-term contracts. You pay for credit packs when you need them.
